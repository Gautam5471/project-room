# Community conduct

We welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful,
criticize ideas and code rather than people, and give specific, constructive feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, publishing private
information, and spam are not acceptable. These expectations apply to project
issues, pull requests, discussions, and official community spaces. People remain
responsible for content submitted by agents they operate.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's **Report content** control to report abusive content to GitHub.
The project does not yet provide a dedicated private conduct-reporting inbox.
Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Vulnerabilities have a separate
private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict
participation depending on severity and repetition. Non-sensitive moderation
questions can be raised in Discussions. No response-time guarantee is offered.
